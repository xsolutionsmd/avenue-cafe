# Design brief

## Visitor job and primary action

Understand this small Towson breakfast/lunch café, explore its actual menu and find it inside the building. Primary action: Call Avenue Cafe at tel:+14102968300. Secondary: exact business map/directions. The selected landing scope was decided before reference search. No custom ordering, reservations, payments or staff form.

## Content plan

- / — Café identity, short breakfast/lunch introduction, prominent call/menu/visit links, one strong real BLT photograph and authentic entrance photography where useful. No invented brand history.
- /menu/ — Full readable offering depth from the actual wall menu photographed July 2026 [S13], organized into breakfast platters, omelettes, breakfast sandwiches/bagels/sides, hot food, house specialties, cold sandwiches, salads, extras and beverages. Include sourced cookies from [S1]. Do not replace the available menu with teaser items. Prices are mostly obscured; publish no prices. One concise call prompt covers current availability/prices.
- /visit/ — Verified street address and phone [S2–S6], exact Maps [S11], authentic entrance photograph. The photo shows signage saying downstairs below PNC building [S14]; use supported lower-level guidance, no invented parking/elevator/accessibility claims. Hours remain conflicting unless direct Maps evidence resolves them.
- Menu ingredients/details come from the actual board, not generated directory descriptions or the rejected Avenue Kitchen & Bar menu.

## Template site

Selected by Astra after scope decision and inspection of the real café assets: https://www.cafececilia.com/index (Café Cecilia).

Inspected desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844, landing, Menus interaction and Info interaction. Café Cecilia provides a polished editorial café presentation, restrained warm paper/black palette, delicate serif body type, generous whitespace, fixed left navigation, inset right content and a text-only printed-menu treatment. Its small-scale café visitor journey—menu and visit/contact—matches ours without requiring a product catalog, elaborate photography archive or invented online services. Its upper landing uses a centered brand mark, then right-column photography/information.

Adopt closely: spacious asymmetrical desktop frame; persistent left navigation about 50px from viewport edge; editorial serif body and italic active states; restrained black/cream type system; right content measure around 628px; text-only menu on cream paper; small footer; quiet transitions; mobile top-right menu with full readable single-column content.

Intentional adaptations:
- Replace the reference's anchor sections with separate Home, Menu and Visit URLs, retaining its navigation and visual rhythm.
- Replace its proprietary pear illustration/logo with Avenue Cafe website lettering and actual café photography. Never copy its assets, typography font files, restaurant copy, proof or identity.
- Avoid the reference's enormous empty mobile landing height; preserve spaciousness while presenting Avenue's name, useful action and real photo promptly.
- Use semantic HTML menu text with category navigation instead of image-only menu scans, supporting phone readability, keyboard and zoom.
- Do not reproduce reservations, shop, cart, press or unrelated hospitality claims.
- No menu-item photos on main/Abdul: reference menu is text-only. After normal delivery, add an evidenced photo variation only to Derek's branch/environment.

Bounded alternatives inspected: https://www.aimeleondore.com/pages/cafe-leon-dore (beautiful green/gold identity but catalog-like isolated drink/product photos exceed our media; fashion navigation unrelated); https://barpisellino.com/ (distinctive identity but oversized cropped mobile emblem and ornamental hero unsuitable here). Café Cecilia best matches the available small authentic library and actual visitor need.

## Accepted decisions and standing rules

Separate pages, at most two complementary font families, no decorative numbering, no fake reviews/ratings, no visitor demo banners, necessary copy only. Preserve native keyboard/focus and reduced motion. Check narrow mobile card/control containment, enlarged text, images/crops and navigation. Do not add feature widgets merely to fill sparse space.

## Remaining uncertainties

Hours conflict, menu prices are not established, exact accessible entrance/parking unknown, no owner-controlled online ordering found. Source/photo dates remain factual limitations. The site can communicate the supported menu and call/directions without filling these gaps with invented facts.
